Migration principles

  1. Preserve original artwork titles, dates and historical context where known.
  2. Do not invent missing credits, descriptions or technical information.
  3. Separate current identity, Ilya Minin (Eli), from the historical ELIAS ADAMS archive without deleting provenance.
  4. Allow one work to belong to several media, thematic or project indexes.
  5. Replace placeholder content with explicit archive statuses such as unfinished, fragmentary or documentation incomplete.
  6. Keep the site lightweight and usable on mobile; new pages should not depend on Bootstrap layout.
