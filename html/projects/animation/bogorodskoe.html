Bogorodskoe

    Experimental Short Film

    2022

    Artist: Ilya Minin (Eli)

    Co-director / collaborator: Yulia Baranyuk

    A video work created around the cultural history and theatrical traditions of Bogorodskoye. The film looks at the relationship between local memory, Russian fairground traditions, music, dancing and the theatrical world that developed around the district.
